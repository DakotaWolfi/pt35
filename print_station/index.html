Station settings







Leave blank to print to a reachable Epson-like device on the dedicated printer network. To pin one printer, enter its MAC address. Detection uses printer hostnames and TCP port 9100; unsupported models may need a manual MAC.
When both discovery fields are set, the printer is found by hostname on Ethernet; its numerical IP can change.




Receipt pictures
Photos fit proportionally within your chosen width and a 1024-dot safety height. 384 dots is about 48 mm at 203 dpi; 576 dots is about 72 mm. Choose a width your paper supports.
Visitor print limits
Default: 6 prints per IP per rolling 60 minutes and a 10-second global cooldown. The cooldown applies across visitors; authenticated NFC owner prints are exempt. Queue capacity stays limited.





Owner print pass
HTTPS administrator only. Generate a temporary code to bypass the display challenge. Share only with trusted people; normal print rate limits still apply.
The pass expires automatically and is separate from the administrator token.
